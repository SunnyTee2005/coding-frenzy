Throwing cards away I

The Problem

A deck of n cards numbered 1 to n is stacked in order, with card 1 on top and card n at the bottom. While the deck has at least two cards, repeat the following: throw away the top card, then move the card that is now on top to the bottom of the deck.

Report the cards in the order they were thrown away, and the single card that remains at the end.

Input

Each line contains one integer n (n ≤ 50). A line containing 0 ends the input and must not be processed.

Output

For each n print two lines. The first line is Discarded cards: followed by the discarded cards in order, separated by a comma and a space (, ), with a single space between the colon and the first card. The second line is Remaining card:, a space, and the last card. Look carefully at the sample for the exact format.

Sample Input

5
6
1
0

Sample Output

Discarded cards: 1, 3, 5, 4
Remaining card: 2
Discarded cards: 1, 3, 5, 2, 6
Remaining card: 4
Discarded cards:
Remaining card: 1
