Hashmat the Brave Warrior

The Problem

Hashmat is a brave warrior who leads his own soldiers into battle. Before every fight he wants to know how many more soldiers one side has than the other, so that he can decide whether to attack.

Write a program that reports the difference between the sizes of the two armies.

Input

The input contains several lines and ends at end of file. Each line holds two non-negative integers: the size of Hashmat's army and the size of the enemy army, in no particular order. No number is greater than 232.

Output

For each input line, print the absolute difference of the two numbers on its own line.

Sample Input

10 12
10 14
100 200

Sample Output

2
4
100
